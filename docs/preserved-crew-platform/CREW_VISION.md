# CREW Technologies

**Coordinated Research, Expertise & Workflows**

## The Idea

Complicated work such as grants, permits, claims, and filings goes wrong in the handoffs. CREW treats it the way a good team would: each specialist has a defined role, each task has a place in the workflow, each handoff has a purpose, and everyone works toward one outcome. A person describes what they need in plain language, and a coordinated team of specialist systems takes it from there. It interviews, researches, produces documents, checks the work, and tracks everything. A human approves every consequential step.

## The Platform

CREW Platform is the shared foundation underneath every product:

- **Intake**: adaptive interviews that fit the person instead of making them fit a form
- **Research**: requirements, rules, and deadlines gathered for the specific case
- **Specialist coordination**: the right expert system for each part of the job
- **Document production**: drafts and forms prepared from verified information
- **Verification**: independent review before anything moves forward
- **Workflow management**: every task visible, with its status and owner
- **Persistent records**: every case saved, so people can leave and come back

## The Products

Each product is a domain-specific application built on the shared platform:

- CREW Grants
- CREW Permits
- CREW Finance
- CREW Estate Planning
- CREW Emergency Management
- CREW Business Services

A new field means a new domain pack on the same platform. It doesn't mean a new product from scratch.
